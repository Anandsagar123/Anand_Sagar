Who I Am

About Me

Curiosity-driven physicist investigating the fundamental structure of nanomaterials.

My research philosophy is deeply rooted in unraveling the intricate structural mechanics of physics-driven nanomaterials and translating them into high-performance applications. I specialize in designing and engineering advanced functional materials for energy storage, environmental purification, and hypersensitive molecular detection.

Currently pursuing cutting-edge materials science research at IIT Ropar, I focus on building sustainable technologies. My passion lies in applying basic physics principles to address real-world challenges in electrocatalysis, plasmonics, and machine-learning-aided spectroscopy.
